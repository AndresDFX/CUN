### Data Availability Statement

**Data Availability Statement / Declaración de disponibilidad de datos / Declaração de disponibilidade de dados.** The system described is an institutional teaching repository that contains student-related administrative data — class rosters, group codes and institutional e-mail addresses — and therefore cannot be published as-is. No research dataset was generated by this work. A description of the architecture sufficient to reimplement it is given in Section 3, and the author will respond to reasonable requests for further methodological detail.

### Declaration on the use of Artificial Intelligence

**Declaration on the use of Artificial Intelligence / Declaración de uso de Inteligencia Artificial.**
Generative artificial intelligence assistants — Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) — were used
in the preparation of this manuscript for drafting and editing prose, and for checking internal
consistency between sections. They were also, and this is the subject of the report itself, used as
the layer-3 component of the system described. No tool generated data, measurements or bibliographic
references that were not individually verified by the author. The author accepts full responsibility
for the content of this manuscript.

**Personal data.** This manuscript contains no personally identifying information about students.
No student record, identifier or work product is reproduced.

**Ethics.** This report describes an authoring system operated by the author and does not involve
research with human subjects. No data were collected from students, and no intervention was applied
to them.
